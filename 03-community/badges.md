# Badges & Milestones

Badges appear on a player’s CatWeb profile.

## Basic badges

| Badge | How to get |
|-------|------------|
| **Site Creation** | Register your first domain (`catweb://domains`) |
| **Site Visitor** | Someone visits one of your sites |
| **Site Visiting** | Visit any site (type a URL in the address bar) |

## Visitor milestone badges

Released in the Anniversary Update (v2.10.0, ~March 2025).  
Background gets darker the higher the tier.

| Badge | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| 10 Visits | ≥ 10 visits on one of your sites |
| 100 Visits | ≥ 100 |
| 1,000 Visits | ≥ 1,000 |
| 10,000 Visits | ≥ 10,000 (very rare — only ~100+ players historically) |

Higher tiers exist / may be added over time.

## Event badges

Limited-time. Usually scavenger hunts or special events (e.g. Hallway / candy events).

## Notes for AIs

- Badges are purely cosmetic / status.
- Visit counts are per-site and visible on profiles.
- Popular high-visit sites (z4.rbx, dotab.rbx, etc.) are how many players earn the higher milestones.
