# Badges & Milestones

Badges appear on a player’s CatWeb profile.  
Data sourced from the Fandom wiki (updated from dump).

## Basic badges

| Badge | How to get | Obtainable |
|-------|------------|------------|
| **Site Creation** | Register your first domain (`catweb://domains` → Register a Domain) | Yes |
| **Site Visitor** | Someone visits one of your sites | Yes |
| **Site Visiting** | Visit any other site (type a URL) | Yes |
| **First Anniversary** | Play CatWeb for over 1 year | Yes |
| **Second Anniversary** | Play CatWeb for over 2 years | Yes |

## Visitor milestone badges

Released in the Anniversary Update (v2.10.0, March 2025).  
Icon backgrounds get darker the higher the tier.

| Badge | Requirement | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| 10 Visits | ≥ 10 visits on one site | Easy with friends / chat |
| 50 Visits | ≥ 50 | |
| 100 Visits | ≥ 100 | |
| 500 Visits | ≥ 500 | |
| 1,000 Visits | ≥ 1,000 | Often needs a known domain or promotion |
| 10,000 Visits | ≥ 10,000 | Very rare (~121 players as of mid-Sep 2026) |

| Badge | Event | How |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Candy Collector | 2024 Halloween | Collect 20 candies (Super Dark Mode) |
| Holiday Haul | 2024 Christmas | Collect 15 presents |
| Ultimate Egg Hunt! | 2025 Easter | Claim all 4 Ultimate Eggs |
| The End? | 2025 Halloween (Hallway) | Reach Door 1000 with ≥20 candy |

## Notes for AIs

- Badges are cosmetic / status only.
- Visit counts are per-site and shown on profiles.
- 10k Visits is one of the strongest status signals in the community.
- Do not encourage alt abuse for visits or free domains.
