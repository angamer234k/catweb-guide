# CatWeb Rules (Summary)

**Source:** Official in-game rules page (`catweb.rbx/en/rules` and language variants) + Fandom wiki.

Violating the rules can get your site taken down and you banned for 14 days.  
False bans can be appealed via site recovery request.

## Core principles

- Everything you create must follow **Roblox Terms of Use** and **Community Standards**.
- You remain responsible for the content you publish.
- CatWeb staff do not endorse user-generated content.

## Sites you create must NOT:

- Impersonate CatWeb or other entities to mislead players
- Use Donations / purchase buttons in a malicious way to scam players
- Abuse exploits or bugs in the editor or its API
- Contain hateful, discriminatory, or offensive content

## Content guidelines

Keep sites respectful. Avoid hate speech, discrimination, scams, or anything that violates Roblox’s standards.

## Notes for AI agents

When generating sites, stay far away from anything that could look like:
- Impersonation of official CatWeb UI or domains
- Fake donation / transfer buttons that mislead
- Any exploit-related or filter-bypass patterns beyond the documented decoder (and only for long legitimate text)

If a user asks for something that clearly violates the above, refuse or heavily sanitize.
